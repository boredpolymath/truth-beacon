Discord Bot Setup Guide & Troubleshooting

Comprehensive walkthrough, permissions checklist, and answers to common configuration questions

Private & Sovereign Discord Integration

TruthBeacon does not require third-party cloud hosting or bot sharing. Follow these 4 steps to configure your own private bot application running securely on your local workstation.

Discord Dev Portal

Core 4-Step Setup Walkthrough

Estimated time: ~3 minutes. You only need to perform this setup once.
1

Create an Application in the Discord Developer Portal

TruthBeacon operates locally on your machine using your own private Discord Bot. You do not share access with any third parties.

  1. Navigate to the official Discord Developer Portal ↗ and log in with your Discord account.
  2. Click the blue "New Application" button in the upper-right corner.
  3. Give your application a clear name (for example: TruthBeacon Guard or Community Guard) and agree to Discord's Developer Terms.
  4. In the left sidebar, click "Bot".
  5. Click "Reset Token" (and enter your 2FA code if prompted). Click "Copy" to copy the bot token. Keep this safe!
2

Enable Privileged Gateway Intents (CRITICAL)

REQUIRED

Discord requires explicit authorization for bots to receive member join and profile update events. Without this step, Discord's Gateway will reject the connection with Opcode 4014 / Disallowed Intents.

How to enable: On the same "Bot" page, scroll down to the "Privileged Gateway Intents" section. Toggle "SERVER MEMBERS INTENT" to ON (green). Then click "Save Changes" at the bottom of the page.
3

Generate Bot Invite URL & Add to Your Server

Generate an official Discord OAuth2 invite link to bring your bot into your church or community server with necessary moderation privileges:

  1. In the left sidebar of the Developer Portal, go to "OAuth2" → "URL Generator".
  2. Under Scopes, check only: bot.
  3. Under Bot Permissions, check:
    ✓ View Channels ✓ Moderate Members (Timeout) ✓ Kick Members ✓ Ban Members
    (Or check Administrator if you prefer a single master grant).
  4. Copy the generated URL at the bottom, paste it into your browser, choose your server from the dropdown, and click Authorize.
  5. Role Hierarchy: In your Discord Server Settings → Roles, ensure the bot's role is positioned higher than newcomer and general member roles so it has the authority to act.
4

Find & Copy Your Discord Server ID

TruthBeacon needs to know which server it is designated to protect:

  1. In your desktop Discord client, open User Settings (the gear icon near your name).
  2. Go to Advanced (under App Settings) and toggle "Developer Mode" to ON.
  3. Close settings. Right-click your server's icon in the server list on the left side of Discord.
  4. Click "Copy Server ID" at the bottom of the right-click menu.
  5. Paste that 18-to-19 digit number into the Target Discord Server (Guild) ID field in Discord Setup and click Test Handshake & Permissions.

Frequently Asked Questions & Troubleshooting

Common questions, edge cases, and role hierarchy guidelines

Why does pre-flight check say "Missing Privileged Intent"?

Discord requires explicit authorization for bots that receive member join events. Open your Developer Portal application, navigate to the Bot tab, scroll down to Privileged Gateway Intents, and ensure SERVER MEMBERS INTENT is enabled.

Why does a "Ban Imposter" action fail with 403 Forbidden?

Discord enforces a strict Role Hierarchy. A bot cannot moderate any account whose highest role is equal to or higher than the bot's own role. In Discord Server Settings → Roles, drag your bot's role near the top of the list.

Is our Discord Bot Token ever transmitted to a cloud server?

No. TruthBeacon has a zero-cloud-tracking, 100% sovereign policy. Your token is encrypted directly into your operating system's hardware Keychain (Apple Keychain on macOS, DPAPI on Windows). It is never sent to any external telemetry or logging service.

What is the "Automatic Safety Brake" (Circuit Breaker)?

To prevent accidental mass moderation or runaway scripts, the Circuit Breaker trips if more than 5 moderation actions occur within 60 seconds. It puts the daemon in a temporary safety pause so operators can review the queue before resuming.
